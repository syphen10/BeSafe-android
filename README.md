# Be Safe

A personal safety app for Android.

- **SOS:** hold the button for 2 seconds to text your trusted contacts a link to your location.
- **Check-in timer:** heading home or meeting someone new? If you don't tap "I'm safe" in time, your contacts are alerted automatically.
- **"I'm safe now":** a follow-up message so your contacts can stand down.
- **Works offline:** alerts use plain SMS and your phone's GPS, so they work without mobile data or Wi-Fi. No accounts, no servers.
- Free, supported by banner ads that never appear on the SOS or check-in screens.

Built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. Requires Android 8.0+.
