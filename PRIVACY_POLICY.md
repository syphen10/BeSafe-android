# Be Safe — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

Be Safe is a personal safety app. It lets you send an SOS text message, with your location, to people you choose, and it can alert them automatically if you don't check in on time. This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it.

**In short: Be Safe has no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything stays on your phone, except the text messages you choose to send to your own trusted contacts.**

## Information the app uses

| Information | Why it's used | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Your name** (optional, typed by you) | Included in alert messages so your contacts know who needs help | Stored only on your phone; included in the SMS you send |
| **Trusted contacts' names and phone numbers** (chosen by you) | To know who to text when you send an alert | Stored only on your phone |
| **Your location** | Added to alert messages as a map link so your contacts can find you | Read only at the moment an alert is sent; sent only inside that SMS; never stored by the app or sent anywhere else |
| **Message history** | Lets you see which alerts were sent, when, and to whom | Stored only on your phone |
| **Number of check-ins that ended safely** | Decides when to show the rating prompt (see below) | Stored only on your phone |

When you pick a contact from your phone book, Be Safe receives only the single name and number you selected. It does not read the rest of your contacts.

## Text messages (SMS)

Alerts are sent as ordinary SMS messages through your phone's SIM card, only to the trusted contacts you added, and only when:

- you press and hold the SOS button,
- a check-in timer you started runs out and you don't respond to the "Are you safe?" warning, or
- you tap "I'm safe now" after an alert, to let your contacts know you're OK.

Your mobile carrier handles delivery, as with any SMS, and standard SMS charges may apply.

## Letting a contact know

After you add a trusted contact, Be Safe can offer to tell them. If you choose "Send a text", the app opens your own messaging app with a short note already written (that you've added them, plus a link to Be Safe on Google Play). You see the message and decide whether to send it. Be Safe does not send this message itself.

## Ratings

After a couple of check-ins have ended safely, Be Safe may ask Google Play to show its standard rating window. This window is provided and controlled by Google Play, and any rating or review you leave is handled by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Be Safe never sees your rating, and never asks for one after an alert.

## Location

Be Safe asks for location permission so it can include where you are in an alert. During an active check-in timer the app runs a visible foreground service (shown as a notification) so it can still get your location if the timer runs out while your phone is locked. The app does not track your location over time, keep a location history, or use location for any other purpose.

## What Be Safe does not do

- It does not have an internet connection permission, and does not upload any of your information.
- It does not collect analytics, crash reports or advertising identifiers.
- It does not sell your information, or share it with anyone other than the trusted contacts you choose to alert.
- It does not read your messages, call history or full contact list.

## Keeping and deleting your information

All information is stored privately on your device and is not included in device backups. You can remove contacts and clear your message history inside the app at any time. Uninstalling Be Safe deletes everything it stored.

## Children

Be Safe is not directed at children under 13.
